## What I do

- Summarize what changed and why.
- List capability-level effects and payload compatibility.
- Report verification outcomes and residual risk.

## Required Sections

- Scope changed
- Capabilities added/updated/removed
- Verification results
- Known limitations and next action

## Rules

- Keep report concise and factual.
- Include function names and validation outcomes.
- Avoid speculative future items unless explicitly requested.

## Checklist

- Report is sufficient for reviewer to reproduce key checks.
- Risks are explicit and bounded.
